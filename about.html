OUR STORY

2007

Our journey began in the quiet neighborhood of Bocobo, Cavite. In a small kitchen, we started sharing our passion for freshly baked goods with the local community.

2008–2025

As our reputation for quality and taste grew, we found a new home along UN Avenue, where we proudly served the community for 18 wonderful years.

March 2026

We turned a new page in our story and moved to the historic district of Intramuros, bringing our recipes, heritage, and dedication to the craft with us.

Today

Panaderia continues to honor its roots while embracing a new chapter. Visit us in Intramuros and taste the tradition in every bite.
